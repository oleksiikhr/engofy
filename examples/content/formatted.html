Reading Strategies for Learners

Good readers don't stop at every unknown word — they keep going and use context to guess the meaning, then check later.

Before You Read

Skim the text once for the general topic. This gives your brain a frame to hang new vocabulary on, which is much easier than reading cold.

  • Look at the title and any headings first
  • Guess what the text will be about
  • Check your guess against the full article afterwards

Only look up a word if you cannot infer it after two or three tries.
