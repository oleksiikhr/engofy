# Why Vocabulary Sticks

Learning **new words** in context works better than memorizing lists in isolation.

## Two Habits That Help

Spaced repetition and active recall are the two techniques with the strongest
research support, according to [this study](https://example.com/spaced-repetition).

- Review words the same day you first meet them
- Use each word in your own sentence
- Come back to it again after a few days

## A Quick Example

Try reading a short article, then writing *three* sentences using words from
it. That single exercise combines input, output, and recall in one pass.
